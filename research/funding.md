# Funding — instruments, rules, deadlines

Compiled 2026-10-22. Every figure here was read this session. Deadlines change:
re-verify before acting. Nothing in this file replaces reading the call document.

## Reality check first

Funding follows a company, a team and a first paid reference. At 2026-10-22
CZAPLA has none of the three. Every instrument below therefore has a
**prerequisite column** — that column, not the money column, is what to work on.

## EU level

### EDF — European Defence Fund
| Call | Budget | Per proposal | Rules | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EDF-2026-LS-DIS-RA-SMERO-NT (research, SMEs + research orgs) | EUR 35M | max **EUR 5M** | Consortium, coordinator must be an **SME**; research orgs capped at 40% of the grant | SME company, TRL ≥ 4, 2-3 partners in 2-3 member states |
| EDF-2026-LS-DA-SME-NT (development, SMEs) | EUR 30M | max **EUR 6M** | Members must be SMEs; 3 companies from 3 member states | same + a prototype to develop |
| EDF-2026-LS-DIS / LS-DA-DIS (disruptive) | EUR 27M / 29M | — | minimum 2 entities from 2 states | same |
| EDF-2026-DA / DA-EXP / DA-ACC | EUR 422M / 40M / 100M | — | any eligible consortium | prime-level |

- **2026 deadline passed: 29 September 2026, 17:00 Brussels time.** So 2027 is the
  target for EDF.
- Both SME topics explicitly welcome **"add-on actions to adapt solutions
  originally developed for civilian applications and previously not applied in
  defence."** That sentence is the door for a civilian detection product.
- TRL ≥ 4 is required — a working lab-validated prototype, which is exactly what
  `hardware/test-plan.md` is designed to produce.
- Recipients and subcontractors must be EU-based with management in the EU and
  not controlled by a non-associated third country.
- STEP: most 2026 topics award a STEP Seal (Sovereignty Seal) if minimum
  requirements are met.

### EU counter-drone deployment money (this is the market, not a grant)
- **EUR 250M** Counter Drone deployment initiative for critical infrastructure and
  EU external borders (incl. a EUR 250M call for land and maritime border
  surveillance), announced with the EU Action Plan on Drone and Counter Drone
  Security (Feb 2026).
- **EUR 130M** added under the Border Management and Visa Instrument (BMVI),
  announced 5 Oct 2026.
- Plus EUR 169M earlier in 2026 and an initial EUR 250M from Dec 2025 →
  **over EUR 500M** total under Home Affairs funds for surveillance and
  counter-drone technology.
- **Goes to member states and Frontex, not to startups.** Our route is to be a
  supplier under a national deployment, which means integrators and primes.

### SAFE (Security Action for Europe)
Loans for joint member-state procurement. Poland used it to finance SAN
(~PLN 15bn, largely SAFE-financed). Reachable only through the PGZ/APS/Kongsberg
supply chain — i.e. as a subcontractor.

### EIC Accelerator / EIC STEP Scale-Up
Supports dual-use and disruptive defence tech; the STEP Scale-Up scheme was
extended to critical defence technologies. Needs a real deep-tech company with a
strong team and traction. **Year 2+, not now.**

### Innovation Fund / Horizon Europe
Not scoped in this session. Horizon Europe clusters and EIC are the places to
look for a civilian-first detection project; treat as a separate research task.

## Poland

### FENG / STEP — defence and dual-use
- The Ministry of Development Funds and Regional Policy plans **more than
  PLN 1.4bn** in FENG calls for defence and dual-use technologies in 2026.
- **STEP applications expected November 2026**, subject to European Commission
  approval of programme changes.
- Needs: registered company, cost plan, own contribution, partner structure.
- **This is the nearest real deadline.** It is why registering the company matters
  sooner than it feels like it should.

### PARP — "Startup Booster Poland – DeepTech Goes Dual"
- Total allocation **PLN 30M**, and it is an instrument for **accelerators**
  (PLN 10-15M per selected operator, up to 100% funding).
- A startup enters **through the selected accelerator operator**, not directly.
- The programme pushes at least +2 TRL up to minimum TRL 6 (functional prototype
  demonstrated in near-real conditions), then commercialisation.
- At least 30% of participants must be dual-use startups; proposals with ≥50%
  dual-use are favoured.
- **Action for us:** identify the selected operators and register interest /
  apply to their cohorts. Cheap, and the TRL target matches our roadmap.

### Other Polish channels worth a later look
- **Program Ochrony Ludności i Obrony Cywilnej (OLiOC)**, 2027-2031: e.g.
  Podkarpackie alone gets over **PLN 1.7bn**, with ≥54% to protective
  infrastructure. Regional money for safety infrastructure — relevant when a
  regional customer exists.
- **PLN 175M** for drone traffic management and part of **PLN 4bn** for
  capitalising Polish defence companies for anti-drone technology (state budget).

## EEA / Norway Grants

### POLNORIS (NCBR) — the biggest realistic single grant
- **EUR 70M** (≈PLN 297M) total; **EUR 1-1.75M per project**; 36 months.
- **80% funding for enterprises** (20% own contribution).
- Project promoter must be a Polish enterprise or research organisation, with a
  **mandatory research partner from Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein**.
- Thematically open, but must serve an EEA priority (green transition; democracy,
  rule of law and human rights; social inclusion and resilience).
- Projects addressing challenges from the invasion of Ukraine are welcomed.
- **Deadline 17 December 2026, 15:00 CET.** Decisions expected June 2027.
- Note: the "social inclusion and resilience" priority is the plausible hook;
  whether a C-UAS project fits is an open question to test with NCBR.
- **The blocker is the Norwegian partner, not the money.** Finding a Norwegian
  research organisation in ~8 weeks is the whole task.
- Related: the research programme's bilateral fund has also announced network
  funding for cooperation in defence, security and foreign policy.

## VC

- C-UAS startups raised **USD 1.2B across 15 rounds** between Q1 2025 and Q1 2026
  (Fortem USD 25M from Lockheed; Austin Sensing USD 11M seed Oct 2026 led by Bow
  Capital with Draper; Perennial Autonomy/Merops won a USD 500M Pentagon contract;
  CHAOS Industries USD 510M Nov 2025).
- What they fund: a team, a working prototype, and a defence customer.
- D-Fend → Motorola at **USD 1.5B** is the exit story that makes funds interested
  in the sector.
- **Verdict: not available pre-team, pre-prototype.** Do not spend time on it in
  2026. The exception is a strategic/industry investor after a pilot.

## Prerequisite ladder (the actual plan)

| Step | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 1. Working two-node prototype + documented test results | TRL 4 statements; credibility in every application |
| 2. One paid pilot or subcontract with a site/integrator | Reference; VAT-registered revenue; the thing grants ask about |
| 3. Registered sp. z o.o. + accounting | FENG/STEP (Nov 2026), EDF 2027, POLNORIS |
| 4. A Norwegian or Icelandic research partner | POLNORIS (17 Dec 2026) |
| 5. Two partner SMEs in two more member states | EDF SME calls 2027 |
| 6. A pilot with a defence-relevant customer | VC conversation becomes possible |

## Do-not-do list

- Do not apply for anything before the prototype exists. Rejection is permanent
  for that call and burns months of the runway.
- Do not spend money on grant writers before step 3.
- Do not chase VC in 2026.
- Do not promise a buyer capability we have not proven in `hardware/test-plan.md`.
